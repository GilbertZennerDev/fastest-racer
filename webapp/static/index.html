FAQ

Why not just use my sim's built-in telemetry?

Sim telemetry shows you what you did. Fastest Racer shows you the mathematical optimum for your exact car — the target to chase, derived from physics, not from a lap someone else drove.

Is my data private?

The simulation itself runs entirely in your browser — your track and car setups never touch our servers unless you explicitly save them to your account.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — Pro is a monthly subscription with no lock-in, managed through Stripe's billing portal from inside the app.
